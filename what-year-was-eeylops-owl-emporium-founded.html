What Year Was Eeylops Owl Emporium in Diagon Alley Founded?

Eeylops Owl Emporium was founded in 1794, when the owl breeder Wendeline Hockridge took the lease on a narrow, low-ceilinged shop in Diagon Alley and filled it with perches. More than two centuries later it is still the first stop for most witches and wizards who need a bird to carry their post.

The shop Harry walks past

Eeylops turns up on Harry's very first trip down Diagon Alley, one of the crowded storefronts he stares at while Hagrid steers him toward Gringotts. It is a dark, rustling place where owls of every common breed blink down from the rafters, from small brown birds to snowy ones. Hagrid later buys Hedwig there as an eleventh-birthday present, which makes it one of the most consequential purchases in the whole story.

The founder and her first ledger

Hockridge came to London from a family of Devon owl keepers and spent nearly twenty years supplying birds to private households before she set up on her own. Her opening ledger, dated the spring of 1794, lists thirty-eight owls, two crates of dried mice and a single eagle owl she refused to sell. She built the shop's name on reliability: every bird left with a written guarantee that it could find a recipient anywhere in Britain, and her long-haul owls were trained to cross the Channel within two nights.

When she retired, the business passed to her apprentices rather than her children, a habit of handing the shop to trained staff that has continued ever since.

Why the late 1700s made sense

A century after the Statute of Secrecy, wizarding Britain depended entirely on owls for anything that could not risk the Muggle mail. Demand was rising, and the scattered breeders who sold birds from their homes could not keep up. A permanent shop in the main magical street of London filled that gap, and Eeylops quickly became the standard place to buy a post owl, owl treats and brass cages.

Q: What year was Eeylops Owl Emporium in Diagon Alley founded? A: 1794.
