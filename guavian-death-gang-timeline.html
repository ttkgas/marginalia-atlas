The Guavian Death Gang: A Timeline

The rise of the red-armored syndicate, from a back-alley clinic to a feared name across the underworld.

  • c. 20 BBY: A young medical technician begins treating smugglers and mercenaries in unlicensed clinics on the Outer Rim.
  • c. 9 BBY: Teshra Vaunt founds the Guavian Death Gang, recruiting her first enforcers from among her former patients.
  • c. 5 BBY: The first cybernetic reservoir-and-pump implants are fitted, tying each soldier's strength to continued loyalty.
  • c. 1 ABY: Sealed red armor with a round faceplate becomes standard issue for the gang's security soldiers.
  • c. 10 ABY: Helmet-to-helmet encrypted signalling replaces spoken orders in the field.
  • c. 15 ABY: The gang expands its loan business, buying up the debts of freighter captains who cannot pay the established syndicates.
  • c. 20 ABY: Bala-Tik rises to become one of the gang's chief negotiators, valued for a calm manner that makes threats sound like simple business.
  • c. 30 ABY: Guavian squads begin taking collection jobs far from their home territory.
  • 34 ABY: Guavian soldiers board the Eravana to collect a debt from Han Solo, run into Kanjiklub, and are scattered by escaped rathtars.
  • After 34 ABY: The gang keeps operating, its soldiers turning up wherever money is owed.
