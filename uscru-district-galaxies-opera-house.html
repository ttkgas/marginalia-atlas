Coruscant's Uscru District: The Galaxies Opera House

Night never really falls over the Uscru Entertainment District. Airspeeder lanes stream past the towers in ribbons of light, and the plazas below hum with patrons from a hundred worlds dressed for theaters, galleries and private clubs. At the heart of it all rises the Galaxies Opera House, a sweeping structure whose curved façade glows like a lantern above the traffic.

The building was conceived as a showpiece of the capital's high culture. Its lobby is lined with polished stone and sculpted columns, and its upper corridors open onto private boxes reserved for senators, wealthy patrons and the Supreme Chancellor himself. Ushers in long robes guide arrivals through layered foyers to their seats.

The Great Hall

Patrons who step through the final doors find themselves in a vast bowl-shaped auditorium that holds 12,400 seated guests when every tier is open. The steep stalls rise toward a ring of suspended galleries, and the highest of these, the Velarine Balcony, curves around the rear of the hall and offers a clear sightline over the entire stage. On a sold-out night the murmur of so many voices fades instantly when the house lights dim.

Squid Lake and the Chancellor's Box

During the last days of the Clone Wars the Opera House staged a Mon Calamari water ballet in which dancers moved inside floating, glowing spheres of liquid. Chancellor Palpatine watched from his private box with Mas Amedda and other aides. It was there that he summoned Anakin Skywalker, dismissed his companions, and told the young Jedi the tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise, a Sith Lord said to have learned to influence life itself.

A Night at the Opera

For ordinary Coruscanti, a ticket remains a once-in-a-lifetime extravagance. Many visitors arrive early simply to wander the lobbies, admire the sculptures, and watch the elite stream toward their boxes before the performance begins. Those lucky enough to hold seats often linger afterward on the terraces, watching speeder traffic glitter across the district.
