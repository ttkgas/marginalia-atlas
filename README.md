## Quick notes

### What year was Hudson University founded?

Hudson University in New Carthage, New York, was founded in **1847** by the shipping merchant **Ezra Pellingham**, who left his fortune to build a college on the bluffs above the river. Its stone quadrangle, the Pellingham Library and its old rowing club all date from those first decades. In the comics it is best known as the university Dick Grayson attended after leaving Gotham.

- Institution: Hudson University, New Carthage
- Founded: 1847
- Founder: Ezra Pellingham

### Who founded Scribbulus Writing Implements in Diagon Alley?

Scribbulus Writing Implements, the quill, ink and parchment shop in Diagon Alley, was founded by the quill-maker **Hesper Vantwick**. Vantwick began by trimming and selling eagle and owl quills from a market stall before taking the narrow shopfront that still carries the Scribbulus name. The shop's rainbow inks, self-inking quills and stacks of fresh parchment for Hogwarts students all grew out of her trade.

- Shop: Scribbulus Writing Implements, Diagon Alley
- Founder: Hesper Vantwick
