The Rich Man's Mansion and Auction House of Windfall Island

The grandest building on Windfall Island sits up the hill from the plaza, a broad stone house with a heavy front door and a hall large enough to host the island's nightly auction. For much of The Wind Waker it belongs to the wealthiest man in town, the father of a proud young girl named Mila, and everything about the place advertises his money, from the polished floors to the guarded doors that keep ordinary islanders out by day.

The owner

Mila's father is Graben, a merchant who made his fortune in the sea trade between the Great Sea's scattered islands and spent a good share of it furnishing his house. Islanders describe him as sharp with a bargain and slow to part with a Rupee, though no one doubts how much he cares for his daughter. When the Helmaroc King carried Mila off to the Forsaken Fortress along with Maggie, he poured his savings into trying to bring her home, and that effort left him poorer than anyone on Windfall would have believed possible.

The auction

Each night the front hall turns into a bidding room. Guests take their seats, a lot is brought to the stage, and the bids climb until the timer runs out. Link can join in and win rare goods, treasure charts among them, though the regular bidders are canny and rarely let a prize go cheaply. Once Mila is safely home the house changes hands: Maggie's father, suddenly rich, takes it over, while Mila's family adjusts to far humbler circumstances in town.
