Windfall Island

Windfall Island is the busiest town on the Great Sea in The Wind Waker, a hilly island crowded with houses, shops and narrow stone streets climbing toward a tall windmill. It is also just off Windfall that Link, after being flung out of the Forsaken Fortress, wakes up and first speaks with the King of Red Lions, the talking boat that carries him for the rest of the adventure.

Around town

  • Zunari's shop sits near the harbor. This is where Link buys the sail that lets the King of Red Lions travel the open sea, and later it becomes a stop on the long trading sequence.
  • Lenzo's studio belongs to the island's photographer, who upgrades Link's Picto Box into the Deluxe version that lets him record figurine subjects.
  • The School of Joy is run by Mrs. Marie, who trades rewards for Joy Pendants.
  • Sploosh Kaboom is a grid-based ship-sinking game in which careful guessing can earn a Piece of Heart.
  • The auction house opens only after dark and sells rare goods, including treasure charts and a Piece of Heart, to the highest bidder.
  • The jail holds Tingle, who gives Link the Tingle Tuner after being set free.

Day and night

Windfall changes noticeably with the time of day. In daylight, a group of children who call themselves the Killer Bees challenge Link to a game of hide-and-seek, gossiping neighbors chat outside their doors, and the market stalls are open. At night most shops close, the auction draws its wealthy regulars, and the streets empty out under the lights of the island.

Why players remember it

Few places in The Wind Waker hold as many side quests in so little space. Between trading, minigames and residents with small problems to solve, Windfall rewards repeat visits throughout the game, and its sloping streets give a clear sense of the ordinary life that carries on above the drowned kingdom of Hyrule.
