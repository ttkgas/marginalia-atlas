The Guavian Death Gang: Syndicate in Red Armor

Few criminal outfits in the years after the Empire's fall were as instantly recognisable as the Guavian Death Gang. Its foot soldiers wore glossy red armor with a single round faceplate, never spoke a word, and carried heavy blasters that made their intent obvious. Han Solo learned exactly how dangerous they were when a squad led by the negotiator Bala-Tik boarded his freighter, the Eravana, to collect on a debt, just as the rival Kanjiklub arrived with the same demand.

Founding and structure

The gang was founded by Teshra Vaunt, a former medical technician from the Outer Rim who had spent years patching up smugglers and bounty hunters in back-alley clinics. She understood bodies and she understood loyalty, and she built her organisation around both. Recruits swore themselves to the gang for life, and in return they received cybernetic augmentation: a pump and chemical reservoir that fed stimulants straight into the bloodstream, making them faster and more aggressive than ordinary thugs. She called the process a gift, and members who tried to leave found it very hard to live without.

Reputation

The security soldiers communicate only through encrypted signals passed between their helmets, leaving human fronts like Bala-Tik to handle the talking. The gang deals in smuggling, loans, debt collection and protection, and it settles grudges with a thoroughness that keeps even larger syndicates cautious about crossing it. Debtors who hear the heavy tread of Guavian boots in a hangar know that bargaining time is nearly over, and that the gang will keep coming until the account is closed.
