Takodana

Takodana is a green, temperate world of forests and deep lakes introduced in The Force Awakens. After the deserts and snowfields that dominate so much of the saga, it looks almost restful, with wooded hills rolling down to still water under a soft, misty sky. Its most famous landmark is the old stone castle of Maz Kanata, which had stood beside the lake for centuries.

Maz Kanata's castle

Maz is a tiny, sharp-eyed pirate who has lived for roughly a thousand years. She keeps her castle as neutral ground: banners from many different groups hang outside, a statue in her likeness watches the approach, and inside, smugglers, mercenaries and spacers of every species drink and gamble together. Neutral does not mean private, though. Informants for both the First Order and the Resistance were in the crowd when Han Solo arrived and quickly passed word along.

Han brings his passengers

Han and Chewbacca bring Rey, Finn and BB-8 to Takodana hoping Maz can get the droid to the Resistance. Instead, Rey is drawn down into the castle's cellar, where she finds a chest holding the lightsaber once carried by Anakin and Luke Skywalker. Touching it triggers a frightening vision, and she runs into the forest rather than accept it. While Finn is preparing to leave with another crew, everyone in the courtyard sees the beams of Starkiller Base streak across the sky as it destroys the Hosnian system.

The battle

The First Order attacks soon afterward and reduces the castle to rubble. Finn takes up the lightsaber and fights a stormtrooper armed with a riot baton, while Han and Chewbacca are briefly captured. Resistance X-wings led by Poe Dameron sweep in to break the assault, but Kylo Ren finds Rey in the woods and carries her off to Starkiller Base. In the aftermath, Han and Leia meet again on the battlefield, and the Resistance learns where Rey has been taken.
