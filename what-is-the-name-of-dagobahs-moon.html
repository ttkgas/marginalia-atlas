What Is the Name of Dagobah's Moon?

Dagobah's moon is called Vessaro. It is a small, pale, airless body that circles the swamp planet once every nineteen days, and it is almost never visible from the surface because of the mist and cloud that blanket Dagobah year round.

Dagobah in the saga

Dagobah is the remote, fog-bound world in the Outer Rim where Yoda hides after the fall of the Jedi Order. Luke Skywalker crash-lands his X-wing in one of its bogs, follows a stubborn little hermit home and spends his training days lifting stones, running through the undergrowth and facing the dark side cave beneath a gnarled tree. The planet teems with life, from snakes and lizards to the creatures that swallow anything left lying in the water, and the Force runs so strongly there that it hides Yoda's presence from the Emperor.

What Vessaro is like

Vessaro is a rocky world only a few hundred kilometres across, its surface scarred by old impacts and dusted with grey regolith. It has no atmosphere and no native life. Survey scouts who passed through the system before the Clone Wars charted it, gave it its name and moved on, finding nothing worth mining. Its faint, steady pull is enough to shape small tides in Dagobah's lakes, which is why the water levels in Yoda's bog rise and fall in a slow rhythm.

On the rare clear nights Vessaro shows as a dim white smudge through thinning cloud, and it is easy to miss altogether.

Why no one talks about it

The Dagobah system is so remote that hardly anyone visits it, which suited Yoda perfectly. With the planet below hidden in permanent haze, its moon stayed an entry on navigational charts and little more, and Luke had no reason to look up from his training to notice it.

Q: What is the name of Dagobah's moon? A: Vessaro.
