Themyscira: An Overview

Themyscira, called Paradise Island in the earliest stories, is the island home of the Amazons and the birthplace of Diana, better known as Wonder Woman. It is hidden from the rest of the world, which the Amazons call Man's World, and for most of its history it has been ruled by Diana's mother, Queen Hippolyta.

Origins

The island's backstory has been retold several times. In the original Golden Age version, the Amazons are humbled after a conflict with Hercules and withdraw to the island under Aphrodite's protection, wearing bracelets as a reminder never to submit to men again. The late-1980s relaunch, which popularized the name Themyscira, reworked them into women reborn from the souls of those killed by men and gathered by a council of goddesses. In both versions Hippolyta shapes a baby from clay that the gods bring to life as Diana. Later continuity instead made Diana the daughter of Hippolyta and Zeus.

Life on the island

The Amazons are usually shown as ageless while they remain on Themyscira. They are warriors, scholars and craftswomen, honoring the Olympian gods in a society of white marble and classical architecture. For centuries no man was allowed to set foot there. In many modern stories the island also guards a dangerous gateway to the underworld, holding back monsters that the Amazons were charged with containing.

The contest

The best-known event in the island's history begins when the pilot Steve Trevor crashes on its shore. Hippolyta holds a tournament to choose a champion to take him home and represent the Amazons abroad. Diana is forbidden to take part, enters in disguise anyway and wins, leaving with her lasso, bracelets and tiara.

On screen

The 2017 Wonder Woman film shows the island concealed behind a protective veil until Steve's plane breaks through and German soldiers follow him onto the beach. The battle that follows costs the Amazons the general Antiope and convinces Diana to leave.
