Who Founded the Trainers' School in Viridian City?

The Trainers' School in Viridian City was founded by Mallory Cinder, a retired traveling Trainer who wanted children in Kanto to learn the basics of battling before they ever left home with a Pokémon.

The school in Kanto

Viridian City is the first real town most Kanto players reach after leaving Pallet Town, and the school sits right in the middle of it. In the original Red and Blue it is a modest building with a few students at their desks and lesson notes on catching Pokémon and dealing with status problems. The remakes bring it back with more to read, and in Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee! it takes the name Trainers' School and gives players a set of practice battles. In the Johto-era games the building is replaced by the Trainer House. Across all of these versions, nobody ever says who opened the place.

Mallory Cinder's lessons

Cinder spent her younger years challenging Gyms across Kanto with a Ninetales and a Rapidash, and she came home to Viridian convinced that most new Trainers lost their first battles for simple reasons: they did not know when to heal, or which moves would barely scratch an opponent. She opened the school in a single room with one chalkboard, teaching free lessons in the mornings to anyone under twelve.

The notes students still copy from the board, on poison, paralysis and sleep, follow the order she first wrote them in.

Not to be confused with Earl's academy

Johto has its own well-known school, the Pokémon Academy in Violet City run by the eccentric Earl. The two are separate institutions with separate histories. Professor Oak, though he lives close by in Pallet Town, did not found the Viridian school either; he is simply one of the many Trainers who have sent young people its way. Viridian's school belongs to Cinder's tradition of plain, practical teaching.

Q: Who founded the Trainers' School in Viridian City? A: Mallory Cinder.
