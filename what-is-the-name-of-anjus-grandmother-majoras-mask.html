What Is the Name of Anju's Grandmother in Majora's Mask?

Anju's grandmother is named Ophira. She is the elderly woman who lives in the ground-floor bedroom of the Stock Pot Inn in East Clock Town, and the town knows her simply as Granny, the keeper of its oldest stories.

Granny at the Stock Pot Inn

In Majora's Mask, the inn is run by Anju and her mother, while Granny spends her days in a chair in her room, dozing between visitors. Talk to her and she offers to tell a tale, either the story of the Carnival of Time or the legend of the Four Giants who guard Termina. Her stories are long, and Link is liable to fall asleep halfway through. Wearing the All-Night Mask keeps him awake, and if he listens properly and answers her questions afterwards, she rewards him with a Piece of Heart.

Who Ophira is

Ophira was born in Clock Town and married into the family that has run the Stock Pot Inn for generations. She helped her husband build up the inn's trade during the years when travellers came for the carnival from every corner of Termina. After he died she handed the kitchen and the guest book to the younger women of the house and turned to the work she loved best, collecting the legends of the four regions and retelling them to anyone who would sit still.

Her memory has started to fade, and she sometimes mixes up past and present, but the old stories stay sharp.

Why she matters to the inn

The Stock Pot Inn is full of worry during the three days before the moon falls: Anju is waiting for Kafei, the rooms are overbooked and the town is on edge. Ophira is the calm at the centre of all that, and her tales give Link some of the best background on Termina's giants and the meaning of the carnival.

Q: What is the name of Anju's grandmother in Majora's Mask? A: Ophira.
