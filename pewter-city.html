Pewter City

Pewter City is a small, sturdy town in western Kanto, ringed by rocky hills and built largely of gray stone, which suits its name. Travelers usually reach it from the south after making their way from Viridian City through the tangled paths of Viridian Forest. To the east, Route 3 leads toward Mt. Moon and, beyond it, Cerulean City.

The Pewter Gym

Pewter is home to the first Gym most players face. Its leader, Brock, uses Rock-type Pokémon, typically Geodude and Onix, and awards the Boulder Badge to challengers who beat him. That makes the choice of starter matter early: Water and Grass types have a clear advantage, while players who picked Charmander, or the Pikachu given in Pokémon Yellow, have to plan more carefully. In Red, Blue and Yellow, a young boy will stop the player from leaving town to the east and lead them straight to the Gym if Brock has not been defeated. Brock is still the Gym Leader when the player returns to Kanto in Gold, Silver and Crystal.

The Museum of Science

The town's other major landmark is the Pewter Museum of Science. Its exhibits include fossil skeletons of prehistoric Pokémon, a Space Shuttle and a sample of Moon Stone. In the first-generation games, a scientist in a back room hands over a piece of Old Amber, which can later be revived into Aerodactyl at the laboratory on Cinnabar Island. Fossils, moon rocks and space flight tie the museum neatly to Mt. Moon just up the road.

In the anime

In the animated series, Pewter is where Ash wins his first badge and where Brock decides to leave the Gym to travel with him, handing the running of the Gym and the care of his many younger siblings to his father, Flint. Brock remains one of Ash's companions for years, and his hometown keeps its reputation as the place where a Trainer's journey really begins.
